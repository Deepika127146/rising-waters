Scientific Methodology & Principles

🏞️ Macro-Catchment Susceptibility

Assesses baseline catchment susceptibility under seasonal precipitation volumes and climatological conditions, calibrated against verified CWC gauge records and INDOFLOODS observations.

⚙️ End-to-End Pipeline

Uses an integrated scikit-learn & XGBoost pipeline handling median numeric imputation, standard scaling, and one-hot categorical encoding directly in memory without synthetic fabrication.

⚖️ Rigorous Boundaries

Strictly distinguishes baseline catchment susceptibility from real-time dynamic evacuation alerts. Dynamic warnings require live telemetry from official agencies.
